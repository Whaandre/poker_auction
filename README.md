### Poker Auction Rules

#### 1. The Setup
*   **Initial Capital**: Every player starts the game with **$1,000**.
*   **Hidden Card**: Each player is dealt one private **hidden card** that remains secret from others.
*   **The Deck**: A total of **13 auction lots** are prepared, with each lot containing 4 cards from a standard deck.

---

#### 2. The Auction Phase
*   **Structure**: The game progresses through **5 rounds** of bidding.
*   **Bidding**: Players submit **blind bids** for all active lots in the current round simultaneously.
*   **Winning**: The player with the highest bid on a lot wins those cards.
*   **Pricing**: Winners pay the **second-highest bid** amount for the lot they won.
*   **Collection**: Won cards are added to your **earned cards** and are visible to everyone.

---

#### 3. The Guessing Phase
*   **Objective**: After all lots are sold, you must attempt to guess an opponent's **hidden card** (Suit and Rank).
*   **Information**: You can see all opponents' earned cards to help narrow down the possibilities via deduction.
*   **Reward**: Correct guesses allow you to steal half of that target player's Prize Score.

---

#### 4. Scoring & Winning
Your final score is calculated from three components:

1.  **Prize Score**: Based on the strength of your best **5-card poker hand** (using your hidden card and earned cards).
    *   Players are ranked 1st through 8th by hand strength.
    *   The higher your rank, the more points you receive.
2.  **Guess Score**: Points gained from your correct guesses or lost if an opponent guesses your card.
3.  **Money Score**: Every **$1** remaining in your balance counts as **1 point**
